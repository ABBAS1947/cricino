Do not copy another project's applicability decisions. Unknown jurisdiction or
audience means applicability is unresolved, not automatically N/A. Applicable law
and binding requirements cannot be waived by an engineering exception.

Consider security, privacy, authorization, data deletion/retention, accessibility,
consumer protection, child safety where applicable, provider dependencies,
resource limits, recovery, documentation and safe verification targets. These are
prompts for project-specific analysis, not universal obligations or passing rows.
